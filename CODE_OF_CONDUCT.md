# Code of Conduct

## Our commitment

We aim to make participation in the MohallaFlow project respectful, constructive and harassment-free for everyone, regardless of experience level, background, identity, disability, or personal characteristics.

## Expected behavior

- Be respectful, patient and constructive.
- Critique ideas and code, not people.
- Explain technical concerns with evidence and actionable suggestions.
- Respect privacy. Do not publish resident, worker, colony, account or credential data.
- Accept responsibility for mistakes and correct them transparently.

## Unacceptable behavior

Harassment, threats, discrimination, intimidation, personal attacks, doxxing, sexualised harassment, and deliberate publication of private information or credentials are not acceptable.

## Scope and enforcement

This code applies in project issues, pull requests, reviews, discussions and other project-maintained spaces. Project maintainers are responsible for clarifying and enforcing the standards and may remove content, restrict participation or close contributions that violate them.

To report a conduct concern, contact the repository maintainers privately through a channel listed in the repository profile. Do not post private details in a public issue.
